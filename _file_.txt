HOW TO APPY

Step 1: Find the job on Linkedln
Open Linkedln, go to the Jobs tab, and search for your keywords (e.g.,
"Payment Operations Remote" or "Transaction Reconciliation").

Click on a job posting that interests you.

Step 2: Look for the "Apply" button (Don't click Easy Apply)
If a job has a blue "Easy Apply" button, do not click it. That is the
automated shortcut that dumps your resume into a massive pile.

Instead, look right next to it or slightly below it for a button that says
"Apply" (sometimes with a little external arrow icon pointing away from
a square, like this: ).


Step 3: Click through to the company's Career Portal
When you click that regular "Apply" button, Linkedln will open a new
tab in your internet browser and redirect you away from Linkedln.

It will take you directly to the company's official website (usually a page
ending in something like careers. companyname. com or
boards.greenhouse.io/companyname ).

Step 4: Submit your application on their system
Once you are on their official website, you will fill out their form. They
usually use secure hiring software like Greenhouse, Workday, or Lever
(these are trusted, standard company software tools, not scams).

Upload the tailored resume we built for you, fill in your details, and
submit.

The Hurdles That Could Lower Your Chances (And How to Beat
Them)
The Location Filter (Madagascar): Some automated HR systems reject
international applicants if the company only wants to hire locally.

How to beat it: Never lie about where you live, but explicitly state
on your CV or profile that you are available as an Independent
Contractor ready to onboard via Deel or Remote.com. This
completely neutralizes the legal excuse companies use to filter
out applicants from Africa.

Applying to the Wrong Roles: If you apply for "Senior Software
Engineer," you will get rejected immediately because you haven't
worked professionally as a coder yet.

How to beat it: Stick strictly to roles like Payment Operations
Specialist, Transaction Reconciliation Analyst, Risk & Fraud
Operations Associate, or Fintech Support Analyst.
